Nautilus — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026

Nautilus is a single-player puzzle game for Apple Watch. It is designed to collect as little about you as possible — in practice, nothing at all. No data whatsoever leaves your device.

What the app stores

Your scores, best tiles, daily-challenge streak, and game settings are saved locally on your device (and in the app's shared container so the complication and widget can show them). This information stays on your Apple Watch and is not sent to us. We do not operate any servers, and we have no database of players.

What we do not do

Nautilus contains no analytics, no advertising, no third-party tracking SDKs, and no account system. We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell or share any data, because we don't collect any.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
