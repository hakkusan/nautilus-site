How to play

Merging
Tiles combine only when their values are consecutive Fibonacci numbers — 1+2→3, 2+3→5, 3+5→8, and so on. Equal tiles (2+2) and skip-a-step pairs (3+8) don't merge. The one exception is 1+1→2.
The Golden Chain
Line tiles into a staircase and a single swipe sets off a cascade; each merge in the chain scores at a rising multiplier.
Rewind
Turn the Digital Crown to nudge a move back if a swipe goes wrong, or to fast-forward again.
Daily Challenge
Everyone gets the same board each day. Keep your streak going.
